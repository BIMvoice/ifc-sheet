Drawn elements can be copied without the command line: Ctrl/Cmd+C and Ctrl/Cmd+V copy the selection and place it where you click, Ctrl/Cmd+D duplicates it in place with an offset, and a bar under the selection offers Copy, Move, Rotate and Mirror. Each is one undo step, and the new keys are listed in the Keyboard dialog (English and Romanian).
