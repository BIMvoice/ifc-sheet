A detail level per view: coarse, medium or fine. A coarse plan draws walls, slabs and roofs by their outline only, without the boundaries between their material layers, and simplifies the openings (a window keeps its frame and loses its glazing lines); fine draws what a view has always drawn. The level follows the scale unless the view or its template sets one: coarse from 1:100 and smaller, medium between 1:100 and 1:50, fine at 1:50 and larger. It is set under Visibility / Graphics, is saved in the project, and is the same on screen, on sheets, in the PDF and in the DXF.
