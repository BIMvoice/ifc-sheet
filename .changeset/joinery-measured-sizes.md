A door or window whose IFC states no OverallWidth or OverallHeight (a non-parametric window, as many Bonsai exports are) is no longer listed as 0 × 0 in the door and window schedule. Its width and height are measured from its geometry, in the element's own frame (the horizontal rectangle of least area around its body, so a window turned in the plan measures as one along an axis), and printed marked as measured (`≈ 1200 × ≈ 1500`); when the IFC states only one of the two, that one is kept and only the missing side is measured and marked (`900 × ≈ 1500`); the viewport card says so. The engine measures the same way (`measureElementSize`, `joineryScheduleData` given `meshesOf`), and the element views are cut in the same frame. Every cell of the schedule can now be typed over from the sheet as a last resort, the sizes, the total and the count of each level included, with the same editor-only marker and "Reset to model value" as the other cells; a typed size or count reads as figures in the CSV and the XLSX when it is a number. The overrides are kept with the project and printed by the engine too. A cell typed on a group of measured size stays with it when the measurement moves by a few millimetres (re-tessellation, rounding: a measured side within 5 mm, stated sides equal), is kept while the geometry is not loaded, and is reported under "What is missing" when the size really changed. When two measured groups are within reach of one saved size (1200 and 1203 mm), the cell belongs to the nearer group alone, so typing on, reading or resetting one group never touches its neighbour's cell; for that, `joineryOverrideOf`, `joineryCellText`, `setJoineryOverride` and `clearJoineryOverride` now take every live group of the schedule's kind as their last argument, and `scheduleOf` attaches them as `groups` beside `overrides`. A cell saved for the windows of unknown size (`?`) before sizes were measured applies to each measured group of that kind; resetting it on one group keeps it on the others, saved under each one's own size.
