One planner makes the drawing set, whether the Wall starts it or the `DRAFTSET` command runs it. Each group of sheets is made on the smallest paper from A3 to A0 that gives its drawings 1:100 or a more detailed scale, each sheet at the most detailed scale that fits, and the Wall says why under every sheet (a group still larger than the largest paper says so). Elevation planes stand just inside the facades of the building, sections and elevations get a view depth that reaches the far side of the building, and running it again adds only what is missing, as one undo step.
