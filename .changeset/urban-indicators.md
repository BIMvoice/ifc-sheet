Urban indicators for the building permit: a schedule of kind `urban` lists each level's built area (its footprint to the outer face of the walls, courtyards left out) and useful area, the useful area by room category, then Ac, Ad, Ad for CUT, Au, the site area, POT and CUT, with the rules they were measured by printed under the table. Footprints are united in plan from the rendered meshes of the walls, columns and curtain walls with the kernel's 2D booleans; slabs and rooms only decide which enclosed spaces are inside. Ad sums every level, basements included (STAS 4908-85); Ad for CUT leaves levels out per level, by default a basement up to 1.80 m floor to floor (Law 350/2001), and CUT is measured on it. A level is a basement when more than half its height is below the terrain level, which the user enters against ±0.00; ±0.00 is found from the storeys or the building's reference elevation, or entered, so models with absolute elevations read the same way. Ac is the built area of the level containing ±0.00. Au counts every IfcSpace except EXTERNAL (balconies, terraces) and GFA (area-scheme) spaces and spaces that aggregate spaces of their own. The site area is entered in the project drawer or the table's tab, saved with the project, and falls back to the IfcSite's GrossArea; without one POT and CUT print as a dash. GPU-instanced walls are measured from their instanced pieces; a wall, column, curtain wall or slab that still yields no outline is counted per level, and the table marks Ac, Ad, POT and CUT as lower bounds (≥) with a footnote. It places on a sheet, prints and exports to DXF like the other schedules.
