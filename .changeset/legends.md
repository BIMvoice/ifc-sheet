Legends: a generated key of what a drawing actually uses. A legend lists, as a swatch and a name in a titled table, the cut material hatches (and membranes), the layers with their pen, the hatch patterns, the symbols and the annotation types in use — for one view, a sheet and the views on it, or the whole project. It stores what to list and how (categories and their order, columns, row height, swatch width, text style, title), never graphics, so it follows the drawings when materials, layers, symbols or types change. New legend on the Annotations tab (or the Legends folder of the Project Navigator) opens it as a tab with its settings, SVG and DXF export; drag it onto a sheet like a view. Legend configurations travel in the library file. Projects saved before this open unchanged.
