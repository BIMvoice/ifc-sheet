Section and other drafting handles now move by click as well as by drag: click a handle to pick it up, move, and click to drop it. The pointer shows an open hand over a handle and a closed hand while it is carried. Holding Shift while drawing a line, section or elevation keeps it horizontal or vertical (the Ortho toggle is not changed), and the ortho state is written beside the cursor.
