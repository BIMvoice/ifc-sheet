A new version of the model shows only what changed. "Load a new version of this model" in the What changed panel (IfcSheet's top bar) replaces the model and keeps the project, then compares the two by GlobalId and lists, sheet by sheet, how many elements each sheet shows that were added, removed, moved, reshaped, had a property changed or were retyped ("A-101: 3 changes"). A click steps into the sheet, where revision clouds on a Revisions layer mark the new place of each changed element and, for a moved one, the old place too. A view shows a change only where the drawing does: a plan from its depth below the cut to its storey's ceiling, a section or elevation on the side of its cut it keeps and within its depth, a cropped viewport inside its crop. Tags, labels and level marks whose element is gone or changed are listed. The command line takes WHATCHANGED, WHATCHANGED CLEAR and WHATCHANGED ACCEPT, also while another tool is waiting for input. The reference model state is recorded the first time, kept in the project file and a package, and, in the browser's autosave, in IndexedDB apart from the localStorage slot, so a large model cannot fill the slot; ACCEPT takes the model as it is as the new reference.
