Standards packs: the German drawing standard (DIN / ISO 128). "Install standard" offers it next to the Romanian one and installs the pens, hatch weights, lettering, a title block with German captions and the decimal comma in one undo step, and `ifcsheet build --standard de` draws with the same pack. The values are ISO 128 / ISO 3098 series members; none is checked against DIN 1356-1.
