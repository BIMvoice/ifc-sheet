On the Wall, a plan's cut height can be dragged from the dot where it sits (the control now spans the whole ruler, not its top half). A hatched zone already drawn has a card for its pattern, scale and angle, and the hatch tool's scale and angle fields take typed values (an emptied field or "0.5" on the way no longer snaps back). Esc ends the running tool even when focus is in a card's list or number field.
