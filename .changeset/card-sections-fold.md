The sections of a schedule's viewport card (sub-reports and their checks, Content, Filter, Text rows) fold. Each heading is a button that opens and closes its section, and a closed section says in one line what it holds ("Content: 2 rows hidden, sorted by size", "Filter: IsExternal is true", "Text rows: 2"), in English and Romanian. A section with a duplicate or gap warning, or with settings off the default, opens by default; the others start closed. What you open or close is remembered for the session.
