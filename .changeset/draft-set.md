**Draft set.** The **Draft set** button beside New sheet in the Project Navigator, or the `DRAFTSET` command, makes a first drawing set from the model: a plan per storey, the four elevations and two sections through the middle (marked on the ground-floor plan), each placed on a sheet at the largest standard scale that fits, with the title block filled from the model. Plans go on A-1xx sheets, elevations on A-2xx, sections on A-3xx. Sections and elevations get a view depth that reaches the far side of the building. Everything is an ordinary view or sheet to correct afterwards. One Undo removes the whole set, and running it again adds only what is missing.
