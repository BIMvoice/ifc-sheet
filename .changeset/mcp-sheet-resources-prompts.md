Drawing-set tools: MCP resources (`ifcsheet://project/schema`, `ifcsheet://session/project`, `ifcsheet://session/sheets`), three prompts (`prepare_permit_set`, `update_set_for_new_model`, `check_readiness_and_fix`), `dry_run` on `ifcsheet_add_view`, `ifcsheet_place_view`, `ifcsheet_set_title_block`, `ifcsheet_add_schedule` and `ifcsheet_apply_template`, and `annotated_ifc` in `ifcsheet_issue_set`. `ifcsheet_add_schedule` for rooms returns the building total (Au) as `total_row`. An `llms.txt` and an examples section in the README.
