Material takeoff: a schedule of kind `materials` lists every IfcMaterial with how many elements use it and its volume and area, read from the model's Qto quantity sets in SI. A layered element shares its quantities by layer thickness, a constituent set by its fractions. It places on a sheet, prints and exports to DXF like a joinery schedule, and to CSV with formula-guarded cells.
