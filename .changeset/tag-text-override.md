A tag's text can be written over the model value on the drawing (edit the tag's text, as for a free note). The tag keeps its link to its element, the sheet and the PDF print the written text, a ring in the editor (not printed) marks it, and "Reset to model value" takes the model's text back. It is saved in the project, stays on its tag through a new version of the model, and is never written back to the model as the element's mark in the annotated IFC.
