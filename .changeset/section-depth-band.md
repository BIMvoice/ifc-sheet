A selected section mark on a plan now shows how far the section looks: a soft band on its viewing side with one handle on the far edge. Drag the handle to set the section's view depth; the depth in metres is shown beside it while you drag, and one drag is one undo step. Typing a depth in the view properties still works. The section mark itself stays locked to its section.
