A detail of an area. On a plan, section or elevation, the DETAIL command (Design tab) marks a rectangle and makes a new view named "Detail 1 - <view>" that shows that area at about four times the scale, cropped to the rectangle, with the same cut, level and depth as its source. The source gets a dashed rounded mark labelled with the detail's number and, once the detail is on a sheet, the sheet's number (for example 1/A-102), which follows renumbering. The mark is locked to its detail; its grips change the detail's area, and deleting either removes the other. Annotation on the source is not copied. Details are saved with the project.
