Symbols on the Annotations tab: a symbol is a named block of lines, circles, arcs and text around an insertion point. Four ship with the app, drawn in code — north arrow, 5 m scale bar, centre line mark and tag bubble — and Make symbol turns any selection into one of the project's own. Place symbols with a size factor and rotation; paper symbols keep their size at any view scale, model symbols keep their real size. Editing a symbol (Edit symbol to get its shapes back, then Make symbol to redefine it) changes every instance, and the instance's symbol, size and rotation are editable in the properties panel. Symbols are listed in a Symbols tab of the Drafting standards dialog and travel in the library file.
