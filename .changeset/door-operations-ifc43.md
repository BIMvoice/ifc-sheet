Door plan symbols follow the IFC4.3 definitions of `IfcDoorTypeOperationEnum`: REVOLVING_VERTICAL is one leaf pivoting about a vertical axis in its middle (a new `pivot` motion), no longer the four-leaf revolving drum; SWING_FIXED_* draws the leaf over half the opening and the fixed panel over the other half (`fixedPanel`); DOUBLE_DOOR_SINGLE_SWING_OPPOSITE_* swings its leaves the way the enum's figures draw them.
