"Annotate the set" is plain functions in the engine: `commandsFor`, `choicesFrom`, `choicesOfTemplate`, `hasChoices` and `ALL_CHOICES` say which of TAGALL, DIMALL and LEVELALL run on which kind of view, and the annotation presets are `effectiveOptions`, `readAnnotationPresets`, `withPreset` and `withoutPreset`, with no store and no locale.
