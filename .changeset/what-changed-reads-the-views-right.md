What changed in a new model version is told per view more truthfully. On a model placed far from the origin (a surveyed site) the changed elements are now found in the views that show them; they were found in none. A section or elevation lists the changes on the side of its cut it looks at, not both sides; a detail, and a sheet viewport that shows only part of a drawing, list only what is in that part. A view cut along a slanted plane is named as not read. No view gets more than a hundred revision clouds: changes that lie close share one.
