Elevations and sections can be drawn in a coloured style: the visible surfaces are filled with the colours of the model's materials (the IfcSurfaceStyle colour the 3D view shows), painted far to near under the linework, alongside the line and hatch styles. It is a per-view switch under Graphics on the Wall, a view template can carry it, and it prints in the sheet and its PDF, in the app and from the command line. The DXF keeps the lines only. A hatch pattern from the material mapping is not drawn on the surfaces yet.
