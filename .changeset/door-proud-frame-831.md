A door whose frame stands proud of its wall's faces (a casing, an architrave, a wrap-around frame) is drawn in its opening on a plan: the hinge on the wall face and the swing as wide as the void, not the frame. The void's jambs are read where the plan's cut crosses the wall, within the frame's lining of its ends (Refs #831).
