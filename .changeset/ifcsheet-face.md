IfcSheet's own face (concept A, direction #20): in the documentation edition the app opens full screen on the Wall, with a quiet top bar and no ribbon, tree or panels. A stepped-into sheet has a rail of six large tools that bring the chosen drawing forward on white paper to work on, a card that says what the tool does or holds the drawing's cut height or depth, and "Add a drawing" (a storey's plan, a section, an elevation) that hangs the new drawing where its kind belongs. Each sheet on the Wall says why it is at its scale. "More" lays the face aside for the engine's full interface.
