IfcSheet's own face (concept A, direction #20): in the documentation edition the app opens full screen on the Wall, with a quiet top bar and no ribbon, tree or panels. A stepped-into sheet has a rail of six large tools that bring the chosen drawing forward on white paper to work on, a card that says what the tool does or holds the drawing's cut height or depth, and "Add a drawing" (a storey's plan, a section, an elevation) that hangs the new drawing where its kind belongs. Each sheet on the Wall says why it is at its scale. A sheet prints or saves as PDF, or exports to DXF, from where it hangs. There is no way back to the engine's full interface from IfcSheet: what it needs is brought into this face.
