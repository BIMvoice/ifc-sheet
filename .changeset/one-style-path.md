One style path for 2D views (#34). Every face and line of a view now takes its visibility, stroke colour, pen (paper mm), dash and fill from one function in the engine, `viewStyler`, over one pen table, `PEN_TABLE` (the table the printed sheets always used: `heavy` 0.7 mm cut and 0.35 mm beyond the cut, down to `hairline` 0.13 / 0.065; hidden lines 0.18 mm dashed 1.5 / 1). It lays the view's preset (with its property rules), the building material's fill, the category graphics, the phase, the element override, the colour scheme and a linked model's halftone in that order. The drafting view, the sheets, their PDF and DXF, the view's DXF and the command line's PDF and DXF all draw through it, so a view looks the same in each. Sheets in the default style print as before. Sheets now follow the view's preset and its property rules (they ignored them), and a material's fill. The drafting view now shows what prints: the sheet's grey cut fill instead of IFC material colours, the sheet's pens instead of a table of its own, hidden lines black and dashed, and linked models halftone. The command line's sheets gain the app's pens: grey cut fills, the cut, seen and hidden pens with their colours and dashes, and DXF layers named by pen (`VIEW-CUT-050`, `VIEW-SEEN-025`, `VIEW-HIDDEN-018`) in place of `VIEW-CUT` and `VIEW-LINES`.
