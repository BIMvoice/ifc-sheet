Layers and line types you can edit. A layer can now be set not to print: it stays on screen and is left out of the sheet SVG and PDF, and its DXF layer is written as non-plotting. The Layers tab has a line-type editor for your own dash patterns in paper millimetres, and a layer takes one next to the built-in types. A drafted line has its own colour, weight and line type in the properties panel, each overriding its layer and reset on its own; the DXF writes the line on its layer with that pen.
