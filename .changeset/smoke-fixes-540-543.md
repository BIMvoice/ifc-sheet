Four fixes found by a browser test of the app. The PDF or image underlay panel is now on the drawing you open from a sheet, and the underlay is drawn at its scale on screen. A drawing's own detail level (coarse, medium, fine) is set in its card's Graphics section. The pencil on a label placed by Tag everything keeps the text you type (the label shows the amber ring, and "Reset to model value" takes the model's text back); a room's three-line label opens as three lines. In the room and area schedule a long room number widens its column instead of printing over the next one, in the sheet, the PDF and the DXF.
