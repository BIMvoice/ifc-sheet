DIMALL places the figures of its dimension strings so that none sits on another figure, on an extension line or on a section line: a figure that does not fit its span goes above or beside the line, or past the end of the string, with a short leader. A figure moved off its dimension line now shows that leader on any dimension. DIMALL says when a string runs through the arrow or letter of a section mark.
