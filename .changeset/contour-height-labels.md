The major contours of a site plan carry their height (#182). Every fifth contour is labelled at a few spaced points, the figure in the project's length unit and decimals as a dimension's, turned to follow the contour and reading left to right, with the contour broken under it. A label too close to the plan's edge is left out. The live view, the sheets, the PDF, the DXF and `ifcsheet build` draw the same labels.
