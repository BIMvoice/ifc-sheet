TAGALL's room tags keep off each other, off the door and window marks and off the cut, and a leader is at most 12 mm long on paper and crosses no wall but the one it enters its room by. A tag in the way is moved inside its room (whole, else compact), else beside it with such a leader, else cut to its number; in a row of tiny rooms (WCs) the compact tag comes first. A tag clear of all of this is not moved.
