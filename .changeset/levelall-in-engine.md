LEVELALL is a pure function in the engine: `planLevelAll` plans the level marks of a section or elevation from plain inputs (the view's kind and elements, the project's levels, the drafts there, the height a drawing point stands for, styles and ids) and returns the marks to add and its messages as translation keys and parameters. `planLevels` and `LevelShape` are exported with it.
