A stepped-into sheet is worked on in place: the wheel zooms about the cursor and a drag on empty paper pans (Fit brings the whole sheet back); a drawing is chosen with a click, moved with a drag, opened to draw in with a double-click and taken off with Delete. Its card renames it, sets its scale from a longer list (1:1 to 1:10000, with 1:2, 1:75, 1:125, 1:150, 1:300, 1:400, 1:750 and more), holds its cut or depth, and places any drawing or schedule not yet on the sheet. Alt and the wheel over a drawing now move its cut or depth.
