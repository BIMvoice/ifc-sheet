A door's swing and hinge side can be flipped on the drawing from the selection bar, and a dimension's side from its own bar, without touching the model. A flipped door is drawn in the accent colour in the editor, prints flipped on the sheet, PDF and DXF, is kept in the project file by GlobalId across save, reopen and a model update, and can be reset one door at a time or all at once. Flipping and resetting are each one undo step.
