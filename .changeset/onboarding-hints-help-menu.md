IfcSheet shows one-line hints where a feature applies (a chosen drawing can be resized by its edges, the ortho state and its key in a line or section tool, double-click to open a sheet or a drawing), each dismissible and brought back by Settings, General, Show first-use hints. A "?" menu in the top bar opens the keyboard shortcuts, six short how-to pages (English and Romanian), the report or suggest form, the tour and the hints setting.
