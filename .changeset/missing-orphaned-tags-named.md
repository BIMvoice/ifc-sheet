**What is missing** names each tag, label or dimension whose element was deleted from the model, once: its text, the element's last known name and class (read from the recorded model state), its GlobalId, and every sheet that shows its view. Click it to zoom to the annotation on its sheet. **Delete** removes it as one undo step; **Re-attach** goes to it and starts REATTACH, where a click on another element takes the tag or label over. A dimension offers Delete only. Text in English and Romanian.
