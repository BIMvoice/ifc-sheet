The Wall: a permanent tab that hangs the whole drawing set at once, grouped by plans, elevations and sections. A sheet is removed with its cross and stepped into with a double-click; inside it the wheel raises or lowers a plan's cut and moves a section's depth, a tray swaps in another proposed section, and six large tools (dimension, tag, text note, level mark, section mark, symbol) add information to the chosen drawing. The documentation edition opens on it when a model loads.
