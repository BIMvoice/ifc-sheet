DIMALL dimensions a plan in one action: for every outer facade that has openings or cross walls, an inner string (corner walls, cross-wall faces, door and window edges), a middle string between the cross-wall axes and an overall string, stacked 12 mm and then 8 mm apart on paper outside the building. The points come from the walls, doors and windows the plan's cut passes through; an L-shaped or turned building is dimensioned along each of its straight facades. They are ordinary dimensions, one undo step, and running DIMALL again adds only the ones that are missing.
