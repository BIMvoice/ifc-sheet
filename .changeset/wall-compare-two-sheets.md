On the Wall, two sheets can be shown side by side to cross-check them, a plan against its section for one. Press Compare on a sheet's card, then on a second sheet's. Both hang at the same zoom (100 % is the paper size), with a switch to pan them together or apart, a swap button, and Esc or the close button back to the Wall at the zoom it had.
