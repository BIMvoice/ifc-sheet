Two entries in the Wall's top bar: "What is missing" lists what the set still lacks, sheet by sheet (click a finding to go to its drawing, or its fix button to run the one-action command that fixes it), and "Issue the set" makes the multi-page PDF, or the zipped SVG or DXF set, with its drawing list and a recorded issue. Every sheet of an issue is drawn by the same sheet the Wall hangs and printed through the same print path as a single sheet.
