A PDF page or an image can be laid under a drawing to trace old scanned drawings. In a drawing tab, the Underlay button adds a page of a PDF (you choose the page) or a PNG or JPEG picture. Set its scale from a distance you know with UNDERLAYSCALE (click two points, type the distance in metres), move, turn and fade it, and lock it. Lines and dimensions are drawn over it as on any drawing. For each sheet you choose whether the underlay prints; it is not printed until chosen. A DXF of a sheet leaves it out and says so. The picture is kept in the project, reduced to at most 2 MB.
