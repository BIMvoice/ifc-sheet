The Wall copes with a large set. Sheets away from the screen hang as outlines with their number and name, and their drawings are not generated until they come near, nearest first; a drawing once made is kept. A search box on the Wall finds a sheet by number, name or the name of a drawing on it, ignoring case and accents, and takes the Wall to it. A small map in the corner shows every sheet and the part on screen, and appears only when the Wall is larger than the screen; click or drag on it to move there.
