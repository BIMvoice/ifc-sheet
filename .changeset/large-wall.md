The Wall copes with a large set. Sheets away from the screen hang as outlines with their number and name. The drawings of the sheets near the screen are generated first, nearest first; the rest follow in the background, one at a time, while nothing is being scrolled, clicked or typed, and carry on after the Wall is left, so the missing-things check reads every plan. A drawing once made is kept, and opening another project starts afresh. A search box on the Wall finds a sheet by number, name or the name of a drawing on it, ignoring case and accents, and takes the Wall to it. A small map in the corner shows every sheet and the part on screen, and appears only when the Wall is larger than the screen; click or drag on it to move there.
