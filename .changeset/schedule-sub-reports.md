A joinery schedule can be split into sub-reports whose content you choose (#186): doors and windows apart, one per storey, or types picked by hand. Each sub-report is a schedule of its own, placed on a sheet like any other and fitted to the paper the same way (it wraps to its viewport and continues on A-6xx.1… when it is large). Projects saved before sub-reports open unchanged.
