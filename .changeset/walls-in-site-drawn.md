A wall, stair, door or any other element a model contains in its IfcSite or IfcBuilding instead of a storey, but that stands inside the building's frame, is cut and drawn with the building on every plan, elevation and section. Rhino's ASIFC export of HouseZ holds 42 of the house's walls in the site, and its ground floor plan showed only a few wall stubs. The frame, the paper and the scale stay the storeys': a garden wall or a shed beside the house stays out, as before.
