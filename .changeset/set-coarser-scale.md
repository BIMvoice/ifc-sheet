A model too large for A0 at 1:200, such as a large site, is now drawn whole on its sheets: the drawing set goes on down the offered scales (1:250, 1:300, 1:400, 1:500 … 1:10000) to the most detailed one its drawings fit at, instead of stopping at 1:200 and overrunning the sheet. 1:50, 1:100 and 1:200 stay the normal choices, so a model that fitted before keeps its scale. The sheet's scale note says why it is coarser: the drawings are larger than the paper at 1:200.
