A door on a plan sits in its opening (#678). Its symbol was drawn on the footprint of the door's own mesh, which is thinner than the wall it stands in and wider than the hole by its lining, so the hinge, the leaf and the swing arc lay over the wall band. The symbol is now fitted to the host wall: the hinge on the wall face, the jambs at the faces of the void, the swing as wide as the opening.
