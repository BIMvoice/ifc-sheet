On the Wall, the tools beside a sheet offer their one-action twins: under Dimension, "Dimension the whole plan"; under Tag, "Tag everything"; under Level mark, "Mark all levels" on a section or an elevation. Each is one undo step and a second run adds only what is missing; what it did is said in the drawing's command line. "Add a drawing" now offers "Detail of an area": mark the area on the drawing in front and the detail hangs on a sheet of its own (A-901 and on) at the scale it was made for.
