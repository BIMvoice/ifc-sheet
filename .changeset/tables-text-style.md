Schedules, reports and the drawing register follow a project "Tables" text style (#500). Add it in Standards, Text styles, and choose the font (sans serif, serif or monospace, the three faces the PDF can write) and, for serif and monospace, italic (the PDF has no italic Inter); it applies on the sheet, in the PDF and as a "Tables" text style in the DXF. Projects without the style print as before; sizes are unchanged.
