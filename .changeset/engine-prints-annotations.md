`ifcsheet build --project` and the MCP exports print more of what the app drew on a project's views (#479): level marks, leaders, radial and angular dimensions, grid axes, hatches (the built-in patterns and the project's own), circles and arcs, drawn by the layout code the app uses. Symbols, a text's frame or leader and a dimension figure moved off its line are still named on stderr.
